What it is

An Expert Advisor that looks for fourteen Japanese candlestick patterns — engulfing, harami, morning and evening star, piercing line, dark cloud cover, three soldiers, three crows, tasuki gaps, hammer, shooting star — across seven timeframes at once. Each timeframe keeps its own magic number, its own filters and its own position management, so what M30 does never interferes with what D1 does.

The grid is timeframes, not orders

No averaging down, no lot multiplication, no second position opened to rescue the first. When a trade goes wrong it loses what it was sized to lose and the robot moves on. If you are looking for a martingale that prints a perfect curve until it empties the account, this is not it.

Risk decides the lot, not the other way round

You set a percentage of equity; the EA derives the lot from the distance to the stop. If the symbol's minimum lot would force more risk than you authorised, it declines the trade instead of rounding up. Stop loss and take profit sit on the broker's server from the first tick, so a dead VPS does not leave a position unprotected.

Explicit circuit breakers

Automatic shutdown when the daily loss limit is reached, a cap on simultaneous positions across all seven timeframes, a spread filter that refuses to enter when your broker widens, and a forced Friday close at the hour you choose.

Every filter can be switched off

Trading hours and weekdays, an EMA trend filter (with, against, or off), plus optional ADX, CCI, stochastic and Bollinger filters. All independent. A filter you cannot disable is a filter you cannot evaluate.

You can see what it is doing

An on-chart panel shows the state of each timeframe, and a periodic journal report logs open positions, the day's result and the reason a signal was skipped. No black box that only ever says "waiting for signal".

Questions

Do I need a licence key to backtest it?

No. The Strategy Tester and demo accounts run without a key, with every feature enabled and no time limit. The key is only needed to trade a live account.

Which symbols and brokers does it work with?

The presets ship tuned for XAUUSD, which is where the testing work was done. Technically it will trade any MT5 symbol, but you would be tuning it yourself. Any MT5 broker works; on accounts with wide gold spreads the spread filter will simply stop entering, which is the intended behaviour.

Is it prop-firm friendly?

It has a configurable daily loss limit and a cap on simultaneous positions, and it does no arbitrage or latency trading, which is what most firms ban. That said, every firm writes its own rules — read yours and set the limits below their thresholds before you switch it on.

Do I need a VPS?

Yes, unless you leave a machine running Monday to Friday. The EA only trades while MT5 is open and connected. A basic VPS near your broker's server costs €10–15 a month.

How much capital do I need?

€1,000 on a standard account lets it work at 0.5% risk per trade. Below that the minimum lot size distorts position sizing and a cent account is the better option. Bear in mind the longest losing streak in the backtest was nine trades in a row — size for that, not for the good weeks.

Is this a grid or a martingale?

Neither. One position per signal, with a stop loss, and if it hits, the loss is taken. The name refers to the grid of timeframes and patterns, not a grid of orders.

Can I get a refund?

No, and that is precisely why you can test the whole thing before buying. As digital content delivered immediately, the right of withdrawal is waived on delivery of the key under Article 103(m) of the Spanish consumer protection act. If you would rather not commit, start with the monthly subscription.

Can I share the file with a friend?

It will not do them any good: the key is signed against your account number and validates nowhere else. Demo accounts need no key, so if you want to show someone, let them run it on demo.

Do you guarantee profits?

No, and be sceptical of anyone who does. This is a tool that executes a set of rules with discipline. The outcome depends on the market, your broker, your costs and the risk you configure. You can lose money with it.
