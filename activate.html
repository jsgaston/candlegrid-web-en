Finding your account number

  1. Open MetaTrader 5. In the Navigator panel, under Accounts, your account appears as a number followed by the broker's name. That number is what I need.
  2. Check it is the live one. A key cut for a demo account will not validate on your live account. Demo needs no key at all, so there is never a reason to send me a demo number.
  3. Send the form. Your mail client opens with the message already written. All you do is press send.
